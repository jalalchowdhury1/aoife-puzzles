# Aoife Puzzles

A puzzle game for Aoife, built so she meets every kind of puzzle on the WISC-V before she sits it.

- **Play:** https://aoife-puzzles.vercel.app on the iPad. Tap Play. Each puzzle type starts with a
  practice puzzle that is explained out loud and untimed.
- **Level 1 = "Find Your Superpowers"**: three sittings of about 15 minutes (Shapes, Memory and
  Speed, Words). She can stop after any puzzle type; the game remembers where she was.
- Puzzles get harder until she misses two in a row, then the game moves on. She never sees
  right or wrong, just "Next!", and gets confetti and a sticker at the end of a sitting.
- The timed puzzles show a calm countdown bar, like the real test has time limits.
- Sound on: the iPad reads number sequences and word problems aloud.
- **Your side:** after each sitting you get a Telegram message. The hidden page
  https://aoife-puzzles.vercel.app/parent (needs the parent key) shows her relative strengths and
  weaknesses by area. These are comparisons within her own results, not IQ numbers.
- New levels are added over time and start from where she topped out last time.
