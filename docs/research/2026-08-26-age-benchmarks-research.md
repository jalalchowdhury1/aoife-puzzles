# Age-benchmark research digest — 2026-08-26

Gathered via Perplexity Pro (7 threads, saved in the account). This digest is the source
for `lib/engine/benchmarks.ts`. All figures are APPROXIMATE developmental anchors, not
test norms; Wechsler raw-to-scaled tables are proprietary and were never used.

## Working memory (thread: "Developmental norms for children ages 5 to 6…")
- Forward digit span at 5–6: mean ≈ 4 (range 3–5). A reliable 5 ≈ likely >90th pct.
- Backward digit span at 5–6: 2–3 typical; **4 ≈ well above age, broadly typical ~8–10**.
- Digit sequencing (reorder ascending) at 5–6: 2–3; reliable 4 = particularly strong.
- Corsi block forward at 5–6: ~3–4 locations (age 7 mean ≈ 4.1, age 15 ≈ 5.6); backward 2–3.
- Auditory word/object span at 5–6: forward 2–3; reordered 1–2.

## Visual-spatial construction (thread: "Developmental age ladder (ages 3-9) for visual-spatial…")
- Block/mosaic copying: age 3 ≈ 2×2 color match; age 4 ≈ 3–4 blocks, 2×2 solid / simple 3×3;
  age 5 ≈ 4–6 blocks, 3×3 mosaics typical, simple 4×4 stretch; age 6 ≈ 6–9 blocks, 4×4 simple;
  age 7 ≈ 8–12 blocks, 4×4 comfortable; age 8 ≈ 4×4 complex, 5×5/6×6 stretch; age 9 ≈ 5×5+.
- Mental assembly (choose pieces to complete a shape without physical trial):
  4½–5 = two pieces w/ modest transforms; 6–7 = 2–3 pieces dependable; 8–9 = multi-piece planned.
- Mental rotation: emergent 3–4; functionally usable 5–6 (simple 2-D); fairly reliable 6–7;
  robust 8–9. Mirror discrimination lags rotation.

## Pattern & rule reasoning (thread: "Developmental age ladder (ages 3-9) for pattern and rule…")
- Repeating: AB 3–5 (dependable 5–6); ABC 4–6; AAB/ABB 4–6 (dependable 5–7); longer units 5–7+.
- Two attributes, same cycle: 5–7. Two attributes, DIFFERENT cycles: 7–9+ (ages 5–8 averaged
  57% on hardest same-vs-different-unit items vs 91% on aligned ones).
- Growing/add-one: concrete 3–4; missing terms 5–6; independent +1/+2 6–7.
- Interleaving (two independent streams): typically 7–9+; mirrors/palindromes 5–7 onward.
- Matrices (RCPM, n=947 ages 6–11): identity/similarity near ceiling at 6 (10.76/11);
  closure/progressive change 8.15/15 at 6 → 14.13/15 at 11.5; abstract analogy 1.88/10 at 6.
- Latin-square/sudoku: 2×2 = 4–5; 3×3 one-of-each w/ choices = 5–7; 4×4 single-line = 6–8;
  4×4 row+column intersection = 7–9+; multi-step = 8–10+; 5×5+ = 9+/experience-driven.
  Preschool study (n=118, 54–77 mo, picture 4×4 row/col, 60 s/puzzle): 66–77 mo mean 3.64/6.

## Quantitative substitution & arithmetic (threads: substitution + CCSS mapping)
- Two-step substitution (1A=2B, 1C=2A → 1C in B): NOT typical at 5–6; 6-year-olds ~61–70% on
  transitive endpoint tasks; reliable ≈ 7–8; curriculum grades 3–4.
- Word problems: add within 10 = K–1; one-step within 20 = gr 1–2; two-step within 100 incl.
  time/money = gr 2–3; ×/÷ in stories with remainder interpretation = gr 3–4 (CCSS 4.OA.3);
  multi-step with fractions of groups / rates = gr 4–5. (Grade ≈ age: K=5–6 … gr5=10–11.)

## Verbal ladder ages 4–13 (thread: "Age ladders (ages 4-13) for verbal knowledge…")
- 5: taxonomic pairs w/ clear labels; concrete high-frequency cloze; seasons/weather basics;
  safe-adult social answers. 6–7: function/category sorting + explanations, school-language
  cloze, days vs months, coins, accident-vs-intention. 8–9: both taxonomic+thematic with ease,
  world-knowledge cloze, clocks 8–10, fractions/units, communication+compromise. 10–11:
  abstract/common-rule groupings, inferential cloze, curriculum systems knowledge, conditional
  social solutions. 12–13: abstract categories, tone/idiom cloze, cross-domain knowledge,
  trade-offs/escalation reasoning. (Matches the banks' authored age-6→13 ramp, decision #10.)

## Processing speed (thread: "Processing-speed and rapid visual-matching norms…")
- **No defensible public items-per-minute norms** — Pearson raw-to-scaled tables proprietary.
  One illustrative sample report point: Bug Search raw 50/120 s (≈25/min) ≈ 95th pct at age 6 —
  a different task from ours; DO NOT convert. Speed genres therefore carry NO age bands in the
  app; they display as her own trend only.

## Gifted-profile context (thread: "In gifted children (top 2-5 percent)…")
- Spiky profiles common; NAGC accepts strength-based composites (incl. EGAI — GDC's index);
  in the 390-child gifted WISC-V sample FSIQ was uninterpretable for most due to scatter;
  modal gifted pattern peaks VCI/VSI/FRI with PSI lowest. "Average is not a deficit."
